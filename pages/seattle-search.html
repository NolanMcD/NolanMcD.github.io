04 / LIFE OUT THERE

Build a realistic landing plan.

Start with a budget and commute, then narrow neighborhoods once a workplace and schedule are clearer.

01

Estimate the monthly cost

Compare take-home pay with rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, insurance, debt and savings. Add moving costs and an emergency cushion separately.

Test the commute
