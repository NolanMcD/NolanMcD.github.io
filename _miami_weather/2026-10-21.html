(Thursday night through Tuesday)
Issued at 208 AM EDT Wed Oct 21 2026

Forecast gets interesting as we head into the weekend and into early 
next week. A 500 mb low will form in the vicinity of Cuba and strong 
surface high pressure will be in place over the northeast US. Deep 
moisture will be in place over SE FL for multiple days with PWAT

the area.

.HYDROLOGY...
Issued at 100 AM EDT Wed Oct 21 2026 

We're beginning to come out of an ebb in the astronomical tides 
as we head towards the full moon and peak of the tide forecasts 
late this weekend and into early next week. Observed water-levels 
are already over-performing predictions and we'll likely begin 
reaching Minor flooding thresholds this morning at all of our 
east coast tidal stations. It's likely we'll also reach Moderate 
flooding levels by as early as Friday but certainly over the 
weekend; even before the peak of the King Tide cycle. It's not out
of the question that Palm Beach county may even reach Major 
flooding levels at the peak of the King Tide. The normal King 
Tides are complicated by the fact that we'll be dealing with a 
complex upper/surface low pressure pattern that will feature an 
approaching front from the north and convection rising out of the 
Caribbean from the south. This will likely increase the onshore 
wind component and magnify the already high tides. This is 
somewhat accounted for in the predictions mentioned above, but 
only crudely. Furthermore, the potential for heavy rainfall during
periods of high tide is also something to be aware of that could 
further aggravate coastal flooding. In short, we're heading into 
what could be a rather impactful period for coastal flooding and 
sensitive locations should consider preparations.
