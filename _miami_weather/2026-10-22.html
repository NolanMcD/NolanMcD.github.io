.HYDROLOGY...
Issued at 106 AM EDT Thu Oct 22 2026

We're beginning to come out of an ebb in the astronomical tides 
as we head towards the full moon and peak of the tide forecasts 
late this weekend and into early next week. Observed water-levels 
are already over-performing predictions and we'll likely begin 
reaching Moderate flooding thresholds this evening at all of our 
east coast tidal stations. It's also not out of the question that
Palm Beach county may even reach Major flooding levels at the 
peak of the King Tide. 

The normal King Tides are complicated by the fact that we'll be 
dealing with a complex upper/surface low pressure pattern that 
will feature an approaching front from the north and convection 
rising out of the Caribbean from the south. This will likely 
increase the onshore wind component and magnify the already high 
tides. This is somewhat accounted for in the predictions mentioned
above, but only crudely. Furthermore, the potential for heavy 
rainfall during periods of high tide is also something to be aware
of that could further aggravate coastal flooding. In short, we're
heading into what could be a rather impactful period for coastal 
flooding and sensitive locations should consider preparations.
