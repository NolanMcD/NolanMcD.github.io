in the low to mid 80s.

shift, the local pressure gradient will start to relax for the 
latter half of Wednesday as mid-level troughing advecting across the 
central US helps to weaken the stout high pressure in the northeast 
US, although breezy northeasterly winds are still expected to linger 
through Wednesday across South Florida. The low near the Bahamas 
will be the primary forcing mechanism for showers and storms, with 
our region sitting on the western periphery of the circulation. 
Enough vorticity and additional forcing should provide enough energy 
for scattered showers and some thunderstorms across the region. Most 
of this is projected to be of the weaker variety with low chances

After fruitful talks with the wind, an agreement has been reached

agreement regarding the evolution of the cut-off mid level low over 
the Bahamas. This feature should finally eject east into the 
Atlantic by end of the day Thursday. This will allow for ridging

weekend. Guidance is hinting that 500 mb heights may climb into the 
588 to 591 dam range, which would be above the 75th percentile for 
this time of year. This pattern should result in temperatures 
warming back up into the mid to upper 80s across South Florida and 
overall less unsettled weather. But, with PWATs hanging around at 
1.6 to 1.8 inches, PoPs will still be around 30% to 40% across most

southwest Florida, closer to the Gulf breeze convergence. Overnight,

Atlantic coastal areas. Luckily for you trick or treaters on
