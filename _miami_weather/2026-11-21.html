.SHORT TERM...
(Today through Sunday)
Issued at 121 AM EST Sat Nov 21 2026

Early morning analysis shows an upper level shortwave over the 
region with the trough axis to our west over the eastern Gulf. 
Meanwhile a surface frontal boundary extends from the eastern Gulf 
cutting up through the center of the state and extends out into the 
western Atlantic, with multiple weak low pressure systems along the 
front. 

With light winds, clearing skies, and some low level moisture in 
place, some fog will be possible early this morning over the 
interior, with areas of locally dense fog most likely over portions 
of the southern Everglades. Will need to monitor this closely this 
morning to see if any headlines or statements will be needed.

The shortwave will lift up to the northeast today and global models 
and forecast soundings show above normal PWAT values continuing 
across South FL. With mid to late day diurnal instability, some 
scattered showers and thunderstorms are expected. While the flow is 
generally unidirectional, 500 mb temps will be in the bottom 10th 
percentile, and MUCAPE values in the middle of the afternoon will be 
around 2k J/kg. So an isolated strong to marginally severe storm is 
not out of the question, with large hail and strong wind gusts being 
the primary threat. Also, given the saturated soils across the east 
coast metro, any heavy rainfall will have the ability to create some 
urban and poor drainage issues, so WPC has a marginal risk for 
excessive rainfall today. High temperatures today will be in the low 
to mid 80s. Convection should quickly wane late tonight with the 
loss of daytime heating. Partly cloudy skies overnight with the 
potential of some patchy fog over the interior. Low temps will range 
from the upper 60s to lower 70s. 

On Sunday the shortwave will continue lifting to the northeast well 
away from the area and the upper level flow across the state will 
become more zonal. Some drier air will begin to filter into the area 
however some widely scattered showers will remain possible. High 
temps on Sunday will range from the low to mid 80s along and north 
of Alligator Alley, to mid and upper 80s south of the Alley.
