Early signs of the developing system have started to appear over

intensifying short-wave embedded trough moving out over the gulf 
over the course of today. A the anticipated low pressure center is
expected to form near the northwest tip of Cuba by morning before
making a slow march north east towards South Florida. 

Ensemble guidance, the European and Canadian global along with the 
REFS high-resolution, is beginning to converge on a preferred track 
of the low pressure that takes it over South Florida by mid 
afternoon today. A notable outlier is the American ensemble which
still prefers a faster moving low pressure track. There is still 
some variance in the track within the slower moving solutions with
60% taking the low over Southeast Florida while 40% take the low 
center over Central Florida. 

Both scenarios favor thunderstorm development through the morning 
today and an increasing severe potential for the afternoon. The 
latest outlook from the Storm Prediction Center agrees with this and 
has bumped Southeast Florida up from a 'Marginal' to 'Slight' severe 
threat. SPC has also increased the tornado probability to 5% over 
Southeast Florida, specifically over Miami-Dade and Broward counties 
with a 2% risk for the remainder of Southeast Florida. Uncertainty 
this afternoon still revolves around the orientation and strength of 
the 850mb jet in conjunction with how high the afternoon CAPE values 
climb to while under the warm sector of the approaching low.The 
upper end of rainfall value ranges for today have steadily climbed 
with each update from ensemble guidance. Wide spread 2-4 inches for 
all of South Florida with localized areas seeing up to 5-7 inches 
and 10+ possible as a worst case scenario. 

Moving into Thursday, rainfall will tail off Wednesday night into 
early Thursday morning and should be completely out of the area by 
Thursday afternoon. High temps for Thursday will be in the mid to 
high 70s with the lows creeping down to the high 60s as a cool and 
dy airmass moves over South Florida.
