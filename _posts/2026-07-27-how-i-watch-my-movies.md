Letterboxd is great at showing me *what* I watch. I also wanted to know *how* I watch it. I went back through my viewing history and sorted every movie by where it came from: a theater, a streaming service, a collection, or somewhere else entirely.

The result is **1,365 viewing records** across five main categories and 21 individual sources.

The individual services tell their own story. HBO Max leads the paid platforms, Criterion reaches an even 100, and movies watched on planes make up the biggest source of free streaming. My own collection supplied another 53 movies—and seven entries came from reading the screenplay rather than watching the finished film.

However I found them, all of these routes led to the same place: another movie logged.
