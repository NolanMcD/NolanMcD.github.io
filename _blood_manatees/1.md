A security camera looks down upon a row of workers in a modern, open-floor-plan office. Around each employee’s face, an AI tracks whether they are currently working. At the end of the row sits EWEN, a young, bearded twenty-something with long hair neatly tied back in a bun. He wears nice business clothes, but nothing too fancy. The AI box around his face is red as he looks down at his phone, clearly not working.

A reverse angle behind Ewen’s shoulder, almost like a third-person video game—or more like he’s being watched. The collar of his white shirt is dirty. His computer screen shows a Slack message from “Zhang Hou.” The person’s job title is Team Lead, and he has no profile picture, just a white silhouette on a gray background. The message asks Ewen to go to the conference room on the seventh floor.

Ewen pockets his phone and picks up the keys on his table, along with another phone—this one branded with a stylized lowercase “i,” with a human eye as the dot. He heads for the elevator, and the camera follows him from behind.
