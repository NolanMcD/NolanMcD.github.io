<p class="bm-transition bm-transition-opening">FADE IN:</p>

A construction crew works in the light of the setting sun.

The orange glow of the sun illuminates a WORKER as he climbs down a ladder to the waterline.

He latches himself to the ladder with a climbing bracket.

The worker uses a rivet gun to unscrew the bottom-left bolt on the galvanized steel covering.

He grabs the bolt and leans over to the opposite side to repeat the process.

Something in the water blows out a breath of air right under his hands as he goes to take the next bolt out.

This startles him, and he drops the bolt into the water, making a loud splash.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">SUPERVISOR</p>
  <p>Hey! Careful! Every bolt you drop is another one the divers have to pick up!</p>
</div>

A dark shadow swims away.

The worker looks at the slowly moving water around him.

The shadow of the bridge, in combination with the reflecting sun, makes it difficult to see under the surface.

He scoffs and unhooks his bracket. Just then, a pillar of water shoots into the sky next to him.

The worker is frozen as the disturbance in the water quickly rushes toward him.

It strikes the ladder, knocking the worker under the surface.

He doesn’t resurface.

A plume of red blood slowly pulls out with the tide as it mixes with the orange hue of the sunset.

<p class="bm-transition">FADE TO:</p>
