Twelve chairs. No one else.

Ewen takes the one facing the wall screen. Sets his company phone and keys together on the table.

The door clicks shut.

The white silhouette from Slack fills the screen. Beneath it: **WORKFORCE TRANSITION AGENT**.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Good morning, Ewen. Would you like someone present?</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Is Zhang available?</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>You’re speaking with Zhang.</p>
</div>

Ewen looks at the empty chairs.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Then we’re all here.</p>
</div>

His employee photograph appears. **HEAD OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE**.

Below it, the three green checks from his desk.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Following the successful transfer of your responsibilities, your position has been selected for elimination.</p>
</div>

Ewen sits back. Lets a moment pass.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>My position.</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>That is correct. This is not a performance action.</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>I’d like that in the agreement.</p>
</div>

**INVOLUNTARY SEPARATION — POSITION ELIMINATED.**

A document opens. Ewen scrolls past the first several pages on the table’s touch panel. Stops at **EXECUTIVE SEPARATION BENEFITS**.

Eighteen months’ base salary. Current-year bonus paid in full. Unvested equity accelerated.

He reads the equity line twice.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>And the transition payment?</p>
</div>

A small wheel turns.

**AUTOMATION HANDOVER INCENTIVE — PAYABLE ON COMPLETION.**

The total increases.

Ewen brings a hand to his mouth. Rubs his beard.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>You may take a moment.</p>
</div>

He lowers his hand.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Has anyone signed off on this?</p>
</div>

Three approvals populate the margin. Personnel. Finance. Compliance.

The same timestamp on all three.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>All required approvals are complete. No further review is scheduled.</p>
</div>

He signs.

**AGREEMENT EXECUTED. PAYMENT SCHEDULED.**

His personal phone lights up. A copy of the agreement has arrived.

Only now does he slide the company phone and keys across the table.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Your access will expire in four minutes. Thank you for helping us build a more efficient workplace.</p>
</div>

Ewen stands. Tucks in the back of his shirt.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>You’re welcome.</p>
</div>

On the security feed, the box around his face turns gray.

**SEPARATION COMPLETE. EXCEPTIONS: 0.**

He holds the door for an employee coming in with a laptop. She gives him an absent nod and takes his chair.
