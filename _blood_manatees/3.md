The conference room is empty.

Ewen steps inside. The door closes behind him with a soft electronic click.

At the end of the table, a wall-mounted screen wakes. A plain white silhouette appears against a gray background—the same blank image used by Zhang Hou on Slack.

Text beneath it reads: **WORKFORCE TRANSITION AGENT**.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Is Zhang joining us?</p>
</div>

A pleasant synthetic voice answers immediately.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>No human attendance is required for this meeting.</p>
</div>

Ewen remains standing.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Your recent productivity, attention, and engagement scores have fallen below the threshold for continued employment.</p>
</div>

On the screen, three red graphs descend beside Ewen’s employee photograph.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Wait. You’re firing me?</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Your employment has been concluded. This decision was generated automatically and has been reviewed for procedural accuracy.</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Reviewed by who?</p>
</div>

The silhouette continues to smile.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">TRANSITION AGENT</p>
  <p>Please leave your company keys on the table. Your building access will expire in four minutes.</p>
</div>

Ewen looks up at the security camera in the corner of the room.

The red box around his face turns gray.
