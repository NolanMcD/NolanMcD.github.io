The lock turns. Ewen comes in, already undoing his cuffs.

His personal keys land in a shallow dish. His phone goes beside them, the separation agreement still on its screen.

He closes the agreement. Pockets the phone.

He passes through a small, orderly apartment. Pulls a long wheeled equipment bag from beneath the bed. Opens it flat.

Inside: empty compartments, rolled dividers, fastening straps laid out straight.

He strips off the office shirt. It drops into a laundry basket. He’s already pulling a running shirt from the shelf.

— A drawer of CELL PHONES. Different sizes, different generations. Ewen wakes each screen with his thumb, checks the charge, switches it off. Each slips into its own numbered sleeve. His everyday phone stays in his pocket.

— Two LAPTOPS close in quick succession. One slim, one thick and scuffed. He slides them into padded dividers, then tucks a matching charger beside each. The cables are already coiled.

— A wooden BASEBALL BAT. An aluminum one. He checks the grips, fits both into the long side of the bag and cinches two straps around them. A practiced tug. Neither moves.

— MARBLES pour from a glass jar into a drawstring pouch. One escapes across the counter. His other hand is waiting at the edge. He drops it in, pulls the cord and nests the pouch between rolled socks. The glass rattle stops.

— At the sink, he fills the reservoir of a RUNNING HYDRATION BACKPACK. Squeezes out the air. Seals it. A short pull on the drinking valve brings water through the tube. He clips the hose against the shoulder strap.

— Clothes roll tight. Fit around the equipment. Nothing has to be forced.

— He closes one compartment, then the next. His hands move ahead of the cuts.

**END MONTAGE.**

Ewen shoulders the water pack. Raises the equipment bag upright and extends its handle.

For the first time, he stands still.

His eyes go once around the apartment.

He reaches toward the keys in the dish. Straightens the dish with his other hand as he takes them.

The bag rolls over the threshold. He switches off the light.

The door closes. The music cuts with the latch.
