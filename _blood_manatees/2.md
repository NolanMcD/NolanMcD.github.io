A security camera looks down a row of desks. Green rectangles follow the workers’ faces. When someone looks away from a screen, a rectangle turns amber.

At the end of the row sits EWEN, a bearded twenty-something with his long hair tied neatly back. Good shirt. Dirty collar.

His rectangle is red. He’s on his phone.

Beside his keyboard, a company badge: **EWEN — HEAD OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE**.

On his monitor, an internal dashboard:

**AUTONOMOUS OPERATIONS — HANDOVER COMPLETE.**

Finance. Personnel. Compliance. A green check beside each.

One window remains open beneath it. An organization chart. Ewen’s name occupies a box above the three departments.

He drags the last line connecting his box to theirs onto **AUTONOMOUS OPERATIONS**.

His box stands alone.

**ROLE REDUNDANCY IDENTIFIED. INCLUDE IN NEXT REVIEW?**

He clicks **INCLUDE**.

A Slack notification covers the chart.

**ZHANG HOU — TEAM LEAD.** No photograph. A white silhouette on gray.

*Please attend Conference Room 7.04. Your presence is required.*

Ewen checks the time. Locks his screen.

The woman at the next desk glances at his notification.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">COLLEAGUE</p>
  <p>Seven?</p>
</div>

He nods.

She takes a breath, about to say something. An amber glow catches Ewen’s eye on her monitor: **ATTENTION CHECK**.

She turns back to work.

Ewen pockets his personal phone. Collects his keys and a second phone, branded with a lowercase “i,” a human eye for the dot.

He leaves a half-full coffee on the desk.

We follow him toward the elevator from slightly above and behind. The security view tracks with us.
