**PASSENGER BOOKING KIOSK — CUSTOMER SECURITY CAMERA.**

On the recorded customer display: **MIAMI VIA NEW YORK / TOMORROW / €980 / CHECKED SPORTS BAG INCLUDED**.

Ewen confirms without opening the cheaper dates.

An offer appears beside his itinerary: **PREVIEW YOUR STAY**.

He selects it.

**MIAMI BEACH HOTEL — LIVE TERRACE CAMERA. NO AUDIO.**

A timestamp in the corner. **04:38 EDT**.

Beyond the railing, the sea is black. The terrace tiles shine under the lights. Two guests sit directly in front of a large fan, their shirts damp at the neck. Nobody is in the uncovered chairs.

A worker drags a squeegee across the tiles. Rain begins again before he reaches the far side.

The guests rise and hurry indoors. Water drives beneath the awning. A lightning flash blanks the picture.

It returns to empty chairs.

A tracking box searches between them, then closes around the worker as he retrieves the fan.

**PASSENGER BOOKING KIOSK — CUSTOMER SECURITY CAMERA.**

The preview ends. A still image replaces it: blue water, white umbrellas, people smiling in the sun.

Ewen selects the live view again.
