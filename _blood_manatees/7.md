    <p>The officer asks whether he has equipment. “Some,” Ewen says. The baggage record shows personal electronics and sporting equipment: phones, two laptops, two baseball bats, and a pouch of glass marbles.</p>
    <p>“You play?” the officer asks. “Sometimes.” The cursor rests beside SPORTING EQUIPMENT. The officer accepts the description.</p>
    <p>The record clears. On the exit camera, Ewen pulls his equipment bag through the doors. His tracking rectangle disappears, but his name remains in the workstation's recent-arrivals list. Below it: OBSERVATION CONTINUES.</p>
