**DUBLIN TO SHANNON INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT.**

A boot closes on the equipment bag.

Ewen settles behind the wheel. His personal phone sits in the dashboard mount. On its screen: **SHANNON AIRPORT**.

He starts the route. Pulls away.

**MOTORWAY CAMERA — NAAS.**

High above the carriageway. A car enters the frame. Crosses it. Leaves.

The camera stays where it is.

**INT. EWEN’S CAR - CONTINUOUS**

Dashboard light catches his beard. The passenger seat is empty.

He loosens his shoulders. Turns the music up.

**MOTORWAY CAMERA — NEWBRIDGE.**

<figure class="bm-route-frame">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/screenshots/toshannon2.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Grainy M7 Newbridge traffic-camera view facing east, showing lane markings and the edge of the carriageway." width="640" height="480" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Visual reference / M7 Newbridge / looking east</figcaption>
</figure>

Another fixed view. Tighter. Headlights wash the lane markings white.

A car passes too quickly for us to see the driver.

In a corner of the feed, a small rectangle appears around its number plate.

A string of characters. Then the rectangle vanishes.

**MOTORWAY CAMERA — KILDARE.**

<figure class="bm-route-frame">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/screenshots/toshannon3.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Dark M7 Kildare traffic-camera view facing east, with reflective road studs receding into the distance." width="640" height="480" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Visual reference / M7 Kildare / looking east</figcaption>
</figure>

Road studs stretch into blackness.

For a moment, nothing moves.

Two headlights surface at the far end. We wait for them.

**INT. EWEN’S CAR - LATER**

Ewen sings half a line with the music. Misses the next one.

His phone dims. He leaves it alone.

Beyond the windscreen, a gantry approaches. He passes beneath it without looking up.

**MOTORWAY CAMERA — M7.**

<figure class="bm-route-frame">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/screenshots/toshannon4.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="M7 traffic-camera view at night, showing bright glare on the central barrier and reflective lane studs." width="640" height="480" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Visual reference / M7 / central barrier</figcaption>
</figure>

Glare sweeps along the barrier.

The car exits one feed.

CUT TO another. It enters.

The music continues across the cut, thin and distant now. No road noise reaches us.

**MOTORWAY CAMERA — LIMERICK TUNNEL APPROACH.**

<figure class="bm-route-frame">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/images/screenshots/toshannon5.jpeg' | relative_url }}" alt="Limerick Tunnel South 1 traffic-camera view, showing a divided roadway, directional markings and a foreground sign." width="640" height="480" loading="lazy" decoding="async">
  <figcaption>Visual reference / Limerick Tunnel South 1</figcaption>
</figure>

A curve in the road. The car disappears behind the foreground sign, then comes out on the other side.

The same number-plate characters appear in the corner.

**EXT. SHANNON INTERNATIONAL AIRPORT - BEFORE DAWN**

Ewen follows the signs for parking.

At the entrance barrier, he lowers his window. Reaches toward the ticket button.

The barrier rises before he touches it.

He draws his hand back. Drives through.

Above and behind him, a camera holds the entrance in frame.
