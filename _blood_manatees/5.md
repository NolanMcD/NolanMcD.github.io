**RESIDENTIAL ENTRANCE — CAMERA 02. NO AUDIO.**

Ewen emerges with the wheeled bag, the rigid battery case and his hydration pack. He makes a selection on his phone.

A car stops beside him. Its boot opens. Nobody gets out.

He loads the bags and takes the rear seat.

**VEHICLE INTERIOR — PASSENGER SAFETY RECORDING. AUDIO ENABLED.**

The two front seats are empty. On the passenger display: **DUBLIN → SHANNON AIRPORT / €295**.

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">CAR</p>
  <p>Departures?</p>
</div>

<div class="bm-dialogue">
  <p class="bm-character">EWEN</p>
  <p>Cargo terminal.</p>
</div>

He confirms the changed destination. The car pulls away.

**MOTORWAY TRAFFIC CAMERA. NO AUDIO.**

The car passes beneath a gantry.

Another feed, farther west. A later timestamp.

**VEHICLE INTERIOR — 02:41:06 ELAPSED.**

Ewen has fallen asleep against the window. His laptop is open on his knees, its display turned away from the camera.

The passenger screen offers **MIAMI: PLAN YOUR STAY**.

It cycles through waterfront restaurants, boat trips, a hotel pool. He has not entered Miami on this screen.

A notification wakes him. He looks down at the laptop.

For a moment he sits very still. Then he brings the display closer, shielding it from the window glare with his hand.

The car passes a sign for Shannon. He does not look up.

**CARGO FORECOURT — CAMERA 06. NO AUDIO.**

He unloads beneath a floodlight. The car departs.

Ewen disappears through the reception door. The camera holds on the empty space where he stood.

A rectangle remains there for a moment.

Then it disappears too.
